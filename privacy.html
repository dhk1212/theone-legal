Privacy Policy

O1. · Last updated 11 September 2026 · 한국어

This app is built to collect as little about you as possible. We do not ask for your name, email address, or phone number in order to take part.

What we store

Account identifierA random ID generated by us. It is not derived from anything about you.
CountryDetermined from your app store account. Used to decide which draw you take part in.
Age eligibilityWhether you are 18 or over — a yes or no. Your date of birth is used to work this out and is then discarded, not stored.
Device identifierStored only as an irreversible hash. We cannot recover the original from it. Used to detect multiple accounts on one device.
NicknameA name you choose. Shown publicly if you win.
ActivityTickets issued, draws entered, and prizes paid.

What we do not store

Your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, precise age, location, contacts, photos, or any advertising identifier we have chosen not to collect.

Sign-in

You sign in with Apple or Google. They confirm who you are and give us an anonymous identifier. We never see or store your password. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, we never receive it.

Advertising

The app shows advertisements, which fund the prizes. Advertising is provided by Google AdMob, which may process device and usage information to serve and measure ads. Their handling of that data is governed by Google's privacy policy.

On iOS you will be asked whether to allow tracking. If you decline, you will still see ads — they will simply be less relevant. Declining does not affect your ability to take part in any draw.

Winners

If you win, we publish the nickname you chose and your country. We never publish your real name, because we never ask for it. To pay a prize we may need to confirm your identity and age; any document you send for that purpose is reviewed and deleted, not retained.

Draw records

Every draw we run is published permanently, including the number of entrants, the prize, and the cryptographic seed used to pick the winner. This is what allows anyone to verify that a draw was not manipulated. These records contain no information that identifies you.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app, under Account. This removes your sign-in link and your device records, so the account can no longer be accessed or connected to you.

Past draw records remain published, as above. They are kept so that every draw stays verifiable by anyone, and they do not identify you.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the data we hold, to object to its processing, or to receive a copy of it. Since we hold almost nothing that identifies you, most of these are satisfied by deleting your account. For anything else, write to us.

Children

This app is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, where the change is significant, tell you in the app.
